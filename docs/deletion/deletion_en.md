### Delete your account and data

App: **KcalSync**
Provider: **Lars Teuscher (Teuvex)**, Dresdner Str. 153, 01705 Freital, Germany

This page explains how to have your account and the data stored about you deleted, what is deleted straight away, what remains for others and what may still exist for a while.

### Option 1: In the app, straight away

The quickest route. You do not have to ask anyone and you do not have to wait.

1. Open **KcalSync**
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Open the **Danger zone** section
4. Tap **Delete account permanently**
5. Confirm your identity once more — by entering your password for a password account, or by signing in again for Google or Apple sign-in
6. Confirm the deletion

Your account and your data are then removed. The process **cannot be undone**.

### Option 2: By email

If you no longer have access to the app, an informal message is enough:

**support@teuvex.de**

State that you would like your KcalSync account deleted, and send the message **from the email address you registered with**. That is the only way we can be sure nobody is having someone else's account deleted.

We will act on your request without undue delay and at the latest within one month (Art. 12 (3) GDPR).

### Delete only the data and keep the account

If you would like to keep your account but start fresh, you can do so in part: in the app under **Settings → Danger zone → Delete all app data**.

This takes effect **on that device only**. Permanently deleted are your nutrition diary, your weight history, your profile with age, weight, height and sex, your pantry, the store products you entered on this device and your settings — this data exists on the device alone. If you shared a store product with the community, that shared copy remains (see below).

Not deleted:

-   **Shopping lists and recipes.** They belong to the entire household and are stored in the cloud. They reappear after a short while because the app reloads them from there. This is deliberate: otherwise a single device would remove the lists of every household member.
-   **Your display name, your city** and your **Premium status**. They are stored in the cloud and are restored the next time they are loaded.
-   **Your account, your sign-in and your household membership.**
-   **Your community posts and your profile picture.**

If you really want to delete everything, use **Delete account permanently** in the app, or ask us by email (option 2).

The data deleted on the device cannot be recovered. If you have switched on your phone's device backup, an older backup may still exist in your Google account; it is only replaced at the next backup. If you reinstall right after deleting, you may get that older state back.

### What is deleted straight away

- Your sign-in account and your email address
- Your profile: display name, city and profile picture (cloud) as well as age, sex, height, weight and goals (on your device only)
- Your nutrition diary with every meal and nutritional value recorded (on your device only)
- Your pantry data (on your device only)
- The time of your last use, your Premium entitlement record and the counters and records kept for your account (AI use, feedback, reports, promotion codes, welcome email, assignment of your proof of purchase)
- Your household membership

**Where this data is held.** Your account, your profile, your household membership and the Premium record are held in the cloud; we delete those in every case. Your nutrition diary, your weight history, your profile with age, weight, height and sex, your pantry and your settings, by contrast, exist on your device alone. If you delete your account **in the app**, they are removed along with it. If you ask us **by email** to delete it, we cannot reach them – they stay on your device until you uninstall the app.

### What happens to your household

If you belong to a household, your membership is removed. The shared data — the family shopping list and recipes — remains available to the other members. Your entries in the shopping list stay, but lose your name, your identifier and the link to your profile picture. If you were the household administrator, that role is passed on to another member automatically.

If you were the **last** member, the household and its shared data (shopping list, recipes) are deleted.

### What remains for others

**Community contributions.** Recipes and product entries you shared with other users remain, so that those users can carry on using them. As long as your household exists, the author shown is the display name you chose. Once your household is dissolved entirely, the reference to you is removed: the author then reads "Anonym" and the household identifier is deleted. No reference to a person remains after that.

**Product photos.** Photos of products also remain. They show goods, not persons, and the app removes image metadata such as the place of capture before upload.

### What may still exist for a while

**Correspondence.** If you wrote to us through the contact form or the feedback function, that correspondence remains for a while: delivery copies for 30 days, feedback for 12 months. Messages in our mailbox are deleted once they have been dealt with.

**Reports.** If you reported a community contribution, the case is retained as a record. Your identifier and email address are removed from it after 12 months; the entire record is deleted after 36 months.

**Technical logs.** Operating the app produces technical server logs and crash reports at Google. They are deleted according to Google's periods (crash reports currently 90 days). Google logs inputs to the AI for up to 55 days.

**Payments.** We do not store payment data. Purchase and billing of a Premium subscription are handled by the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. Those records are subject to the respective provider's terms.

### Cancelling your subscription

Deleting your account does **not** end a running subscription. You need to cancel that separately, where you took it out:

- **Android:** Google Play Store → profile picture → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions
- **iOS:** Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions

It is best to cancel **before** deleting your account, otherwise billing continues.
