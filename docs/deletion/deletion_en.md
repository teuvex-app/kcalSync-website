### Delete your account and data

App: **KcalSync**
Provider: **Lars Teuscher (Teuvex)**, Dresdner Str. 153, 01705 Freital, Germany

This page explains how to have your account and the data stored about you deleted, what exactly is removed, and what has to remain for a while for legal reasons.

### Option 1: In the app, straight away

The quickest route. You do not have to ask anyone and you do not have to wait.

1. Open **KcalSync**
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Open the **Danger zone** section
4. Tap **Delete account permanently**
5. Confirm your identity once more — by entering your password for a password account, or by signing in again for Google or Apple sign-in
6. Confirm the deletion

Your account and your data are then removed. The process **cannot be undone**.

### Option 2: By email

If you no longer have access to the app, an informal message is enough:

**support@teuvex.de**

State that you would like your KcalSync account deleted, and send the message **from the email address you registered with**. That is the only way we can be sure nobody is having someone else's account deleted.

We will act on your request without undue delay and at the latest within one month (Art. 12 (3) GDPR).

### What is deleted

- Your sign-in account and your email address
- Your profile: age, sex, height, weight, goals
- Your nutrition diary with every meal and nutritional value recorded
- Your personal recipes, shopping lists and pantry data
- Device data and the time of your last use
- Your Premium entitlement record

### What happens to your household

If you belong to a household, your membership is removed. The shared data — the family shopping list, for instance — remains available to the other members. If you were the household administrator, that role is passed on to another member automatically.

If you were the **last** member, the household and its shared data are deleted.

### What remains

**Community contributions.** Recipes and product entries you shared with other users remain, so that those users can carry on using them. Once your household is dissolved entirely, the reference to you is removed: the author then reads "Anonym" and the household identifier is deleted. No reference to a person remains after that.

**Correspondence.** If you wrote to us through the contact form or the feedback function, that correspondence remains for a while: delivery copies for 30 days, feedback for 12 months.

**Reports.** If you reported a community contribution, the case is retained as a record. Your identifier and email address are removed from it after 12 months; the entire record is deleted after 36 months.

**Payments.** We do not store payment data. Purchase and billing of a Premium subscription are handled by the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. Those records are subject to the respective provider's terms.

### Cancelling your subscription

Deleting your account does **not** end a running subscription. You need to cancel that separately, where you took it out:

- **Android:** Google Play Store → profile picture → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions
- **iOS:** Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions

It is best to cancel **before** deleting your account, otherwise billing continues.
